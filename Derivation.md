# Derivation

Inputs: the hydrogen line (length), the triple point of equilibrium hydrogen (temperature, pressure) and a few universal constants (Boltzmann constant k, mass of the H₂ molecule, impedance of free space Z₀).

Equilibrium hydrogen is hydrogen with its ortho/para ratio in thermal equilibrium, which at the triple point is almost pure para-hydrogen. Normal hydrogen (75 % ortho) has a slightly different triple point (13.96 K, 7.2 kPa).

## Base units

Quantity    | Source                                   | Value in SI units
------------|------------------------------------------|------------------
Length      | Wavelength of the hydrogen line          | 0.211061140542 m
Temperature | Triple point of equilibrium hydrogen     | 13.8033 K
Pressure    | Triple point of equilibrium hydrogen     | 7041 Pa
Impedance   | Impedance of free space                  | 376.73 Ω

## Amount of substance: molecules in a cube L³ of hydrogen gas

The number of H₂ molecules an ideal gas would have in the cube at the triple point:

N = P × L³ / (k × T)  
N = 7041 Pa × (0.211061140542 m)³ / (1.380649×10⁻²³ J/K × 13.8033 K) = 3.474×10²³ = 0.5768 mol

This is a definition, not a measurement: real hydrogen vapour at the triple point deviates from an ideal gas by about 1–2 %, but the unit is defined by the formula, so it is exact.

## Mass

M = N × m(H₂)  
M = 3.474×10²³ × 3.3474×10⁻²⁷ kg = 0.001163 kg = 1.163 g

## Force: pressure on a square surface L²

F = P × L²  
F = 7041 Pa × (0.211061140542 m)² = 313.7 N

## Energy

E = F × L  
E = 313.7 N × 0.211061140542 m = 66.20 J

Note that E = P × L³ = N × k × T, the pressure times the volume of the cube.

## Time

T = (M × L² / E)^(1/2)  
T = (0.001163 kg × (0.211061140542 m)² / 66.20 J)^(1/2) = 0.0008846 s

Since E/M = k × T / m(H₂), the unit of speed L/T = (k × T / m(H₂))^(1/2) = 238.6 m/s is the (isothermal) speed of sound in hydrogen gas at its triple point. So the unit of time is the time sound takes to cross the cube.

We could have used the time light in a vacuum takes to travel distance L (~0.000000000704 s), but that number would have been impractically small. As a result the speed of light is not 1 but 1,256,448 LH/TH. This factor can be rounded to a whole number and used to talk about much bigger or much smaller amounts of a given unit. For example, the time unit multiplied by 1,256,448 gives about 18 minutes and 31 seconds. We write this by appending an apostrophe.

1 TH  = 1 t▽  = 0.0008846 s  
1 TH' = 1 t▽' = 18.5 min

## Frequency

f = 1 / T = 1130.5 Hz

## Power

W = E / T  
W = 66.20 J / 0.0008846 s = 74840 W ≈ 75 kW

## Electric units

Power fixes the product of potential and current (V × I = W). The remaining choice, their ratio V / I, is the impedance unit, and the impedance of free space Z₀ = (μ₀ / ε₀)^(1/2) is the natural universal value for it.

V = (W × Z₀)^(1/2)  
V = (74840 W × 376.73 Ω)^(1/2) = 5310 V

I = W / V  
I = 74840 W / 5310 V = 14.09 A

Q = I × T  
Q = 14.09 A × 0.0008846 s = 0.01247 C

(An earlier version used the charge of all electrons in the cube as the unit of charge, which gave 0.6 mV and 1.3×10⁸ A, far outside the human range.)

## Exchange value / Unit of account

A unit of account measures the exchange value of something in units of hydrogen mass of equivalent value. It is not a currency in itself, as currencies also include policies and a way to transfer them.

At the time of writing, a cube of hydrogen gas (about 1.16 g) was worth about $0.0009 or 0.0008 €, which by coincidence can be rounded to roughly one tenth of a cent.
