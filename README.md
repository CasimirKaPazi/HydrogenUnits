Hydrogen Units
==============

This is a thought experiment on what system of measurement that is universal and with only little arbitrary decisions made. So that any civilization in the universe could come up with the same values.

It is based on the physical properties of Hydrogen and Hydrogen gas.

* All base units are derived from properties of hydrogen(gas) or similar universal values.
* All units have to be in a "human range". Neither to small or to big to perceive.
* Independent from numeral systems used. No arbitrary factors.

Symbols
-------

Hydrogen units are represented with two symbols. One to show that it is a hydrogen derived unit, the other to specify which quality it represents.
The hydrogen unit symbol is either H (standard) or ▽ (fancy). The first is the standard symbol for Hydrogen, the later an old alchemical symbol for water (Unicode U+25BD).
The symbols for the qualities are mostly derived from latin. Such as L for "longitudo".
When used with capital H, the letters for quality are uppercase, to differentiate PH (pressure) from pH (acidity or alkalinity).

Examples
--------

1 m		= 4.738 LH

0 °C	= 19.789 °H  
37 °C	= 22.470 °H  
100 °C	= 27.034 °H  

20 °H = 2.9 °C too cold  
21 °H = 16.7 °C nice cool  
21.334 °H = 21.334 °C ideal  
22 °H = 30.5 °C nice warm  
23 °H = 44.3 °C too hot  

Pressure at sea level (Earth)  
101.325 kPa = 14.3907 PH  
Pressure at surface level (Mars)  
0.636 kPa = 0.0903 PH  
Pressure at surface level (Venus)  
9.2 MPa = 1307 PH

Speed of light  
299,792,458 m/s = 1,255,354 CH

230 V = 0.137 VH
