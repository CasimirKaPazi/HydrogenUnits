Hydrogen Units
==============

A thought experiment on a system of measurement that is universal and needs only few arbitrary decisions, so that any civilization in the universe could come up with the same values.

It is based on the physical properties of hydrogen and hydrogen gas.

* All base units are derived from properties of hydrogen (gas) or similar universal values.
* All units should be in a "human range", neither too small nor too big to perceive.
* Independent of the numeral system used. No arbitrary factors.

Quantity            | Symbol | Alt. symbol    | Source                                 | Value in SI units
--------------------|:------:|:--------------:|----------------------------------------|-------------------------
Length              | LH     | l▽            | Hydrogen line                          | 0.211061140542 m
Temperature         | °H     | °▽            | Triple point of equilibrium hydrogen   | 13.8033 K
Pressure            | PH     | p▽            | Triple point of equilibrium hydrogen   | 7041 Pa
Impedance           | ZH     | z▽            | Impedance of free space                | 376.73 Ω
Amount of substance | SH     | s▽            | H₂ molecules in a cube L³ of gas       | 0.5768 mol
Mass                | MH     | m▽            | Mass of those molecules                | 0.001163 kg = 1.163 g
Force               | FH     | f▽            | Pressure × Length²                     | 313.7 N
Energy              | EH     | e▽            | Force × Length                         | 66.20 J
Time                | TH     | t▽            | (Mass × Length² / Energy)^(1/2)        | 0.0008846 s
Speed               | CH     | c▽ = l▽/t▽  | Length / Time                          | 238.6 m/s ≈ 859.0 km/h
Frequency           | 1/TH   | 1/t▽          | 1 / Time                               | 1130.5 Hz
Power               | WH     | w▽            | Energy / Time                          | 74840 W ≈ 75 kW
Potential           | VH     | v▽            | (Power × Impedance)^(1/2)              | 5310 V
Current             | AH     | a▽            | Power / Potential                      | 14.09 A
Charge              | QH     | q▽            | Current × Time                         | 0.01247 C
Exchange value      | UH     | u▽            | Cost of Mass                           | 0.0008 € ≈ $0.0009

To see how they are derived see: [Derivation](Derivation.md)

Symbols
-------

Hydrogen units are represented with two symbols. One shows that it is a hydrogen derived unit, the other specifies which quantity it represents.
The hydrogen unit symbol is either H (standard) or ▽ (fancy). The first is the standard symbol for hydrogen, the latter an old alchemical symbol for water (Unicode U+25BD).
The symbols for the quantities are mostly derived from Latin, such as L for "longitudo".
When used with capital H, the letters for the quantity are uppercase, to differentiate PH (pressure) from pH (acidity or alkalinity).

Examples
--------

1 m = 4.738 LH

°H is an absolute scale (like K):

0 °C   = 19.789 °H  
37 °C  = 22.469 °H  
100 °C = 27.033 °H

20 °H     =  2.9 °C   too cold  
21 °H     = 16.7 °C   nice cool  
21.334 °H = 21.334 °C ideal  
22 °H     = 30.5 °C   nice warm  
23 °H     = 44.3 °C   too hot

Pressure at sea level (Earth)  
101.325 kPa = 14.39 PH  
Pressure at surface level (Mars)  
0.636 kPa = 0.0903 PH  
Pressure at surface level (Venus)  
9.2 MPa = 1307 PH

Speed of light  
299,792,458 m/s = 1,256,448 CH

230 V = 0.0433 VH
